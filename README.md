## Abstract
Sector classifications support asset comparison, valuation, and portfolio construction in traditional financial markets. Applying similar taxonomies to digital assets requires experts to interpret long and heterogeneous official documents, making consistent review difficult to scale. We present SectorLens\footnote{\url{https://github.com/Paul-scpark/AAAI-SectorLens}}, an evidence-grounded, human-in-the-loop system that combines dense semantic retrieval, sector prototype matching, and lexical classification to rank L3 sector candidates with supporting passages from official sources. Its interface connects macro-level taxonomy exploration with micro-level source verification, allowing reviewers to inspect evidence and approve, revise, or defer recommendations. Evaluated on 226 assets across 34 L3 sectors, SectorLens achieves Hit@1 of 0.593 and Hit@3 of 0.792. In a preliminary study, a majority of three domain experts selected the model's sector for 70% of 30 evidence passages from correctly classified assets, indicating that the surfaced evidence is often sector-relevant.
